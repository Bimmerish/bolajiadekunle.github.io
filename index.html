    Hi, I'm Bolaji Adekunle

    Data Engineer | Data Scientist | SQL Server

    Over the past five years I've worked across the public and private sectors, turning data into insight that supports better decisions. My experience includes forecasting and operational reporting for electronic monitoring services across England and Wales, and working with local government data across healthcare, social care, and adult and children's services.

    Most of my work is building and running the pipelines that take data from operational systems into the warehouses and reports people depend on. That means ETL and ELT in SQL Server and SSIS, migrating data between platforms, data warehousing, and modelling data for Power BI. I build pipelines that can be rerun safely, fail clearly when something is wrong, and are easy for the next person to pick up.

    I also use Python and machine learning for forecasting and analysis where they add value, and I'm comfortable with cloud platforms and database administration.

    Alongside my core role, I've supported the Youth Futures Foundation's work in Blackpool, helping improve the local data ecosystem behind efforts to reduce the number of young people who are not in education, employment or training (NEET).
